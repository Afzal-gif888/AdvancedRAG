Site note 59

The remote support team for the Glacier G4 answers tickets during regular office hours and escalates urgent cases. Travel to the Turin office requires a visitor badge and a printed hotel confirmation. Travel to the Zurich office requires a visitor badge and a printed hotel confirmation. The security guard at the Zurich site checks laptop bags only when an alarm has been triggered. The warranty text for older Helix H6 units was archived in the legal folder and is no longer maintained.
